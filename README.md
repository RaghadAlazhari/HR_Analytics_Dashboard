# HR Analytics Dashboard

## Overview

The **HR Analytics Dashboard** provides an insightful analysis of employee attrition and key HR metrics using Power BI. This interactive dashboard helps HR professionals and decision-makers understand workforce trends, identify factors influencing attrition, and optimize talent management strategies.

## Dataset Description

The dataset consists of **38 columns**, capturing various employee attributes, including:

- **Employee ID:** Unique identifier for each employee.
- **Age:** Employee's age.
- **Gender:** Male or Female.
- **Department:** The department the employee belongs to.
- **Job Role:** Specific role of the employee.
- **Education:** Educational background.
- **Years at Company:** Number of years the employee has worked at the company.
- **Salary:** Employee's salary level.
- **Attrition:** Indicates whether the employee left the company (Yes/No).
- **Job Satisfaction Score:** A rating from 1 to 4 representing job satisfaction levels.

## Key Analyses & Insights

### 1. Employee Attrition Analysis
- **Attrition Rate Overview:** Displays the overall attrition rate in the company.
- **Attrition by Department:** Identifies departments with the highest and lowest attrition rates.
- **Attrition vs. Job Satisfaction:** Examines the correlation between employee satisfaction and retention.

### 2. Demographic Insights
- **Age Distribution:** Analyzes the age groups most affected by attrition.
- **Attrition by Gender:** Highlights whether there is a gender-based trend in employee turnover.
- **Education and Attrition:** Investigates whether education level influences attrition rates.

### 3. Salary & Experience Analysis
- **Salary vs. Attrition:** Explores whether salary levels impact employee retention.
- **Years at Company:** Determines how long employees typically stay before leaving.

### 4. Work-Life Balance & Job Roles
- **Attrition by Job Role:** Examines which job roles have the highest attrition rates.
- **Work-Life Balance Impact:** Analyzes whether work-life balance influences employee retention.

## Dashboard Visualizations

1. **Cards**
   - Display key performance indicators (KPIs) in a concise format, providing quick insights into critical metrics.

2. **Donut Chart**
   - A circular chart used to represent proportions of a whole, allowing users to compare categories in a visually appealing way.

3. **Stacked Column Chart**
   - A column chart where each bar is divided into multiple sections, allowing you to visualize the distribution of different categories within a single data series.

4. **Area Chart**
   - A chart that displays quantities over time or categories, with the area under the line filled in to highlight the magnitude of change.

5. **Stacked Bar Chart**
   - Similar to the stacked column chart, but horizontal. It helps in comparing the composition of different categories across several groups.

6. **Slicer**
   - A filtering tool that allows users to interactively select data ranges, helping them focus on specific segments of the dataset.

7. **Matrix**
   - A cross-tabulation visualization that organizes data into rows and columns, useful for comparing values across multiple dimensions.

8. **Treemap**
   - A hierarchical chart that represents data in nested rectangles, showing proportions of categories and subcategories within the whole dataset.
